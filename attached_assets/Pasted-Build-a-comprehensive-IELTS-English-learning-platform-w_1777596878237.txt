Build a comprehensive IELTS & English learning platform with the following structure:

## 1. LANDING PAGE
- Hero section showcasing two main products:
  * LEXO FOR IELTS
  * LEXO FOR ENGLISH
- Features section highlighting platform benefits
- Product cards with "View Details" buttons

## 2. PRODUCT DETAIL PAGES
- Each product has a dedicated page with:
  * Detailed descriptions (text content)
  * Hero images/screenshots
  * Embedded video demonstrations (YouTube embeds)
  * "Enroll Now" call-to-action button
  * Clear value proposition

## 3. ENROLLMENT & PAYMENT FLOW
- Step 1: Student clicks "Enroll Now"
- Step 2: Redirect to payment gateway
  * Support: Tabby, Tamara, Visa/Stripe, Bank Transfer
  * Show product price
  * Display payment options
- Step 3: After successful payment
  * Send confirmation email
  * Generate enrollment receipt
  * Redirect to student dashboard

## 4. STUDENT DASHBOARD
- Display enrolled courses/products
- Show course materials (organized by product)
- Progress tracking (basic)
- View enrollment details
- Account settings/profile

## 5. SIGN UP / AUTHENTICATION
- Simple registration form (email, password, name, phone)
- Email verification (optional but recommended)
- Login system
- Password reset functionality
- Protected routes (only authenticated users can access dashboard)

## 6. ADMIN DASHBOARD
- Secure login (different from student login)
- Manage Students:
  * View all enrolled students
  * Approve/Reject pending enrollments
  * View enrollment status per student
- Communication:
  * Send bulk emails to students
  * Send targeted emails (by course/status)
- Analytics:
  * Total sales revenue
  * Number of enrollments per product
  * Monthly revenue reports
- Applicant Management:
  * Queue of pending applications
  * Approval/Rejection workflow
  * Automated email notifications
- Course/Product Management:
  * Add/Edit product details
  * Update prices
  * Manage product images and videos

## 7. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Stack: Node.js/Express (backend), React/HTML (frontend)
- Database: Store students, enrollments, admin data
- Email Integration: SendGrid or similar (send confirmation & admin emails)
- Responsive design (mobile-friendly)
- Arabic/English language support (bilingual UI ready)

## 8. WORKFLOWS
- Student Journey:
  1. Visit landing page
  2. View product details
  3. Click "Enroll Now" → Payment
  4. Complete payment → Confirmation email
  5. Access dashboard → View course materials

- Admin Journey:
  1. Login to admin panel
  2. View pending students
  3. Approve/manage enrollments
  4. Send communications
  5. Monitor sales & analytics

Start with the landing page and authentication, then payment integration, then admin panel.
Keep the design clean, minimal, and professional (dark theme with gold/teal accents preferred).
