## PHASE 7b - ADMIN UX (Email Notifications)

1. Auto-email student on Verify:
   - Subject: "Payment Verified ✅"
   - Body: "Your enrollment is now active. Access course here: [link]"
   - Include: Course name, access date

2. Auto-email student on Reject:
   - Subject: "Payment Rejected ❌"
   - Body: "Your payment proof was rejected. Reason: [admin reason]"
   - Include: Re-upload link

3. Audit log:
   - Who verified/rejected
   - When
   - Reason (for rejects)
