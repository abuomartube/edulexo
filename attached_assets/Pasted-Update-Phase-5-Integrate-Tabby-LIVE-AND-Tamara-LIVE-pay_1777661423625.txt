### CHECKOUT PAGE

/checkout/[course-id]/[tier-id]

Shows:
1. Course details (name, tier, price SAR)
2. Order summary
3. Payment method selection:
   - "Pay with Tabby" (Tabby logo)
   - "Pay with Tamara" (Tamara logo)
4. "Proceed to Payment" button
5. Terms checkbox

### FLOW

**TABBY:**
1. Select "Pay with Tabby"
2. POST /api/checkout/tabby
3. Redirect to Tabby portal
4. Complete payment
5. Webhook callback
6. Mark enrollment "active"
7. Send email
8. Redirect to dashboard

**TAMARA:**
1. Select "Pay with Tamara"
2. POST /api/checkout/tamara
3. Redirect to Tamara portal
4. Complete payment
5. Webhook callback
6. Mark enrollment "active"
7. Send email
8. Redirect to dashboard
